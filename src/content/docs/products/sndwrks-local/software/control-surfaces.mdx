## What it is
<Icon name="star" />

sndwrks control IRL! Plug a streamdeck into a computer, open our desktop app, and blammo, you can setup and control the surface from the server.

A key can fire a macro, send a chat speed button, or hit the e-stop. A panel plugs into any machine on the LAN, or connects to the network directly, and shows up in the app as something you bind keys on.

Panels adopt the same way a sndwrks sensor does: the panel shows a setup code, you approve it, and the two hold their own certificates from then on.
