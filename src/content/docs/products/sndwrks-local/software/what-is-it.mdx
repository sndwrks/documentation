## TLDR;
<Icon name="star" />

the sndwrks local app runs on our servers and delivers a website to configure the server. The server offers many functions from control to utility.

You could use sACN to control a videohub matrix to switch to a black source for monitor blackouts.

## Where to start
<Icon name="star" />

We'd recommend the [Quick Setup](../quick-setup)

## Why the browser?
<Icon name="star" />

We considered many options, but thought the browser was the best trade off. There's lots of computers on shows with browsers, and this way you can use any browser or OS you'd like.
